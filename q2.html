10 Reasons Why This Website Is Bad

The bad website I picked is arngren.net. It is an online store in Norway that sells electronics, gadgets, and vehicles. It is still online today and it still shows up on lists of the worst websites in 2026.

Why it is bad:

  1. The page is way too cluttered. Tons of products are crammed onto one page.
  2. There is no clear navigation, so it is hard to find anything.
  3. The text is really tiny and hard to read.
  4. The colors are random and there is no concise theming.
  5. There are so many pictures that they all fight for your attention.
  6. It's not mobile friendly. The page does not resize to fit a phone screen.
  7. On a normal computer screen you have to scroll sideways to see everything.
  8. The spacing is messy and things are squished together.
  9. You cannot tell where one category of products ends and the next one starts.
  10. Nothing stands out, so when you first open the page you do not know what the business is or where to look first.
